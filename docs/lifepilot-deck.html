01
The problem
Wellness apps want your
data. That's the business model.
1
Your health, mood, spending, and location get shipped to servers you don't control.
2
A "privacy policy" is really permission to monetize the most intimate data you have.
3
Turn off the internet and most "AI" wellness apps stop working entirely.
LifePilot
02
The solution
Every AI inference runs on-device, via ExecuTorch.
The app works identically in airplane mode — that's not a fallback, it's the whole architecture. The only network calls that exist are opt-in and off by default: a coarse-location weather lookup and optional model updates. Neither ever sends anything about you.
LifePilot
03
What it does
Four on-device models, one calm app
◎
Overwhelm Manager
Llama 3.2 1B · QAT-LoRA · ExecuTorch
Breaks a freeform task into 5–8 calm steps; personalizes from on-device memory. Optional voice input (Whisper tiny.en).
◐
Energy Planner
Trained time-series model
Predicts a 24-hour energy curve from your sleep and activity via Health Connect.
◉
Hydration Tracker
Trained regression model
A personalized daily water target, optionally sharpened by local weather & AQI.
◈
Expense Scanner
On-device OCR + extraction models
Camera or PDF receipt → structured expense. Handles currencies worldwide.

Architecture

Energy .pte
Hydration .pte
Expense .pte
On-device feature stores · sandboxed, encrypted at rest
ModelRegistry — bundled-first, atomic + rollback
Opt-in weather — coarse, grid-rounded location only. Off by default.
Opt-in model updates — model-in only. Nothing about you is ever sent.
Ships a working model per feature that works offline forever, even if no update is ever fetched. React Native (Expo, New Architecture).
