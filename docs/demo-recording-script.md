# LifePilot — Demo Recording Script (synced action ↔ voiceover)

> Target ~2:25. Record the phone screen in ONE clean pass with **airplane mode ON
> the whole time** (that's the proof). Record voiceover separately afterward and
> lay it over — much cleaner than talking while tapping. Each scene below pairs the
> exact ON-SCREEN ACTION with the WORD-FOR-WORD voiceover to speak over it.

---

## Pre-flight (do BEFORE hitting record)
- [ ] Plug in the phone; screen brightness up; **Do Not Disturb ON** (no notifications mid-take).
- [ ] **Airplane mode ON.** Confirm Wi-Fi AND mobile data are both off.
- [ ] Open LifePilot once and let all models finish loading (Overwhelm needs a warm-up).
- [ ] **Dry-run the Overwhelm prompt once** (below) to confirm it produces a clean 5–8 step result, then go back so the input is empty for the real take. (Do ONE generation on camera — it's the most stable.)
- [ ] Have a **receipt ready** — a physical one to point the camera at, OR a receipt PDF saved in Files as a fallback (upload is more reliable than live camera on video).
- [ ] Do NOT tap the microphone button, and do NOT switch Live Weather on (needs network).

**Overwhelm prompt to type (tested-clean, concrete):**
> `plan a small birthday dinner for my friend this weekend`

---

## SCENE 1 — Hook  (0:00–0:12)
**ACTION:** Start on the Home screen. Swipe down the status bar so the **airplane-mode icon is clearly visible** for ~2 seconds, then swipe it back up.
**VOICEOVER:**
> "This is LifePilot — an AI wellness app. Look at the top of the screen: airplane mode is on. Everything I'm about to show you runs entirely on this phone. Nothing touches the internet."

## SCENE 2 — The four features  (0:12–0:22)
**ACTION:** Slowly scroll the four feature cards so all are seen (Overwhelm, Energy, Hydration, Expense).
**VOICEOVER:**
> "Four features — and each one is powered by its own AI model, running on-device. No servers, no account, no data collection."

## SCENE 3 — Overwhelm Manager  (0:22–1:05)  ← the star, don't rush
**ACTION:**
1. Tap the **Overwhelm Manager** card.
2. Type the prompt: *plan a small birthday dinner for my friend this weekend*.
3. Tap generate/send.
4. Let the steps **stream in live** — do not cut. Pause on the finished list.
**VOICEOVER:**
> "Let's start with the Overwhelm Manager. I'll give it a real, messy task."
> *(as it generates)* "This is Meta's Llama 3.2 model, quantized to run right here on the phone's own chip."
> *(as steps appear)* "Watch — it breaks the task into clear, calm steps, generated offline, in real time."
> *(on the finished list)* "And it learns my patterns over time — in memory that never leaves the device."

## SCENE 4 — Expense Scanner  (1:05–1:40)
**ACTION:**
1. Tap back to Home, then tap the **Expense Scanner** card.
2. Scan the receipt (or upload the PDF).
3. Show the extracted fields populating — merchant, amount, currency, category.
**VOICEOVER:**
> "Next, the Expense Scanner. I'll scan a receipt."
> *(as it processes)* "On-device OCR reads it, and trained models pull out the merchant, the total, the currency, and a category — and it handles receipts from around the world."
> *(on the result)* "Still no network. All on the phone."

## SCENE 5 — Hydration + Energy  (1:40–2:05)
**ACTION:**
1. Home → tap **Hydration**. Show the progress ring, the daily target, and the "why today" breakdown. *(Do NOT toggle Live Weather on.)*
2. Home → tap **Energy**. Show the 24-hour energy curve.
**VOICEOVER:**
> "Hydration gives me a personalized daily target from a trained model — and explains exactly why. The only optional network call in the whole app is a weather lookup, and it's off by default."
> *(on Energy)* "And the Energy Planner predicts my focus and wind-down windows from my own activity."

## SCENE 6 — Close  (2:05–2:25)
**ACTION:** Return to the Home screen. Optionally swipe the status bar down once more to show the airplane icon still on, then hold on the calm Home screen.
**VOICEOVER:**
> "Everything you just saw — four AI models — ran with the radio off. LifePilot: intelligence that actually stays yours. Built on-device, trained with AMD compute. Thanks for watching."

---

## After recording
- Trim dead air at the very start/end; keep total under ~2:45.
- Lay the voiceover over the screen recording (any editor: CapCut, Clipchamp — both free).
- If a scene ran long, cut from Scene 5 first (drop Energy), then Scene 4.
- Optional: 1–2 sec title card at the end — "LifePilot · private by construction" — matches the deck's closing slide.
